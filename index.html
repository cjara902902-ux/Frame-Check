        How it works

        How accurate are the FPS numbers?

        They are estimates, not measurements. Each game has a baseline frame rate on a reference PC taken from typical public benchmark results. We scale it for your graphics card, processor, memory, resolution and quality preset. Ray tracing and frame generation are modeled as typical multipliers, and 1% lows are estimated from what limits your PC. Real results can differ, sometimes by a wide margin, especially in unusual scenes, with mods, or on very old drivers. Treat every figure as a rough guide, not a promise.

        Why does my CPU matter as much as my GPU?

        Every frame needs both chips. The graphics card draws it, the processor prepares it. Whichever is slower sets your ceiling. If the CPU is the limit, a faster graphics card will barely change your frame rate, and the advisor will say so.

        What does the upscaling switch do?

        DLSS, FSR and XeSS render the game at a lower internal resolution and rebuild the image. On Quality mode that typically lifts frame rate by 20 to 45 percent depending on resolution. It helps most when your graphics card is the limit.

        How does this site make money?

        From ads and from affiliate and referral links to shops. The upgrade ranking is calculated from estimated frame rate gained per dollar and does not depend on who pays us. Prices are rough guides, so always check the current price with the shop.

        How does Build my PC choose parts?

        It tries every processor and graphics card pairing we can price, adds the motherboard, memory, storage, power supply, case and cooler each one needs, and drops anything over your budget. Of what is left it picks the build with the highest average estimated FPS across all the games at your resolution on High settings. It checks the socket, memory type, power supply size and cooler. It does not check graphics card length against the case.

        Where do benchmark results and leaderboards come from?

        Results you log are stored in your own browser and never leave your device, so the highest-FPS table only shows your results. The best-PC-by-budget table is calculated by the same model as everything else, not submitted by other people. A shared database of real results needs a server, which this page does not have yet.

        Is the assistant an AI?

        No. It is a set of rules that recognises a handful of question types, such as upgrades within a budget, low FPS in a game, whether a part will work, and builds, and answers them from the same data as the rest of the page. It does not use a language model and cannot answer questions outside that.

        My hardware is not in the list

        Choose the closest model. Cards within the same family, such as an RTX 3060 and an RTX 3060 Ti, differ a lot, so pick carefully.
